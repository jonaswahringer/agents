delta-flume: inside one vessel

Every vessel follows this blueprint. Only the purpose, and the policy and mechanism that serve it, differ.

Shapes

Outside the vessel: trigger and output.
Vessel: one responsibility. The purpose layer.
Gateway: input policy.
Agent loop: the mechanism.

Inside the shapes

Decision: policy. A unit moves on only if it passes.
Step: work done by agents, tools or people.
Queue: units wait here.
Exit: a unit leaves without landing.
Kind of part: d deterministic, s stochastic, h human.

Steps

TriggerTyped, or loose and routed by a human through triage.
AcceptJudges the unit. Declines a duplicate without new input, a superseded unit, or incomplete input, and asks for what's missing. Declined units are logged.
StartJudges the vessel. While it's overloaded or paused, the unit waits in the queue. Each vessel picks the order.
EstimateEstimates the unit's size: the spend it is expected to need, in tokens and time. Deterministic when a number exists, such as the lines changed between two dependency versions. Otherwise an agent estimates.
WriteAgents and tools, such as agent CLIs and the runtime, make a change.
VerifyChecks the change, cheapest first (after poteto's pyramid; a well-designed codebase makes every check cheaper):
  • d static analysis, tests, CI
  • d runtime verification
  • s review agent, against prose rules and skills
FindingsEach finding goes back to Write with its reason. None left: on to Land.
WatcherWatches what the loop spends (tokens and time) against the estimate. The loop works alone until Land, and a human can step in at any time. Over the estimate: a human is notified, sooner for heavier units. Stuck (no progress, long silence) or over the hard ceiling, a multiple of the estimate: the unit moves to dead letters, and a human is notified.
LandA human decides by default. A reversible class of work can be allow-listed to land on deterministic checks alone. Changes requested go back into the loop as findings. Rejected output is closed and recorded.
OutputA PR, a report, an issue.
RecordEvery step leaves a trace of source, reasoning and decision. Where it lives is up to the mechanism: a PR, git history, an issue, or a log.
