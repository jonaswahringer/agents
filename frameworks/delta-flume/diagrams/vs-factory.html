One assembly line vs. many vessels

A typical software factory pushes every unit down one line. delta-flume gives each responsibility its own vessel and keeps a human at the doors.

Typical software factory

A spec goes in, role agents hand it down the line, and it ships when the checks pass.

delta-flume

Each responsibility gets a vessel with the same blueprint: gateway, agent loop, land. A human routes loose work in and decides what lands.

Inside every vessel

Shape

FactoryOne line. Every unit passes every station.

delta-flumeOne vessel per responsibility, all on the same blueprint.

Way in

FactoryA spec or a ticket.

delta-flumeTyped triggers go straight to their vessel. Loose ones pass shared triage and a human route.

Who decides

FactoryAgents approve agents. Green checks ship.

delta-flumeAn agent's verdict is a suggestion. A human lands by default.

What lasts

FactoryThe line is built around today's tools.

delta-flumePurpose and policy stay. Agents and tools get swapped as they improve.
