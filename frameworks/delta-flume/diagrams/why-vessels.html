Vessels, not roles

Role-based setups copy a human team: one agent per job title, then more agents to check the agents. A vessel starts from the responsibility instead.

Role-based agents

Agents named after job titles pass work around and judge each other. Who owns the outcome?

Vessel

One responsibility, split into three layers that age at different speeds. Agents are parts of the mechanism.

Starting point

RolesA job title copied from a human team.

VesselOne responsibility, such as keeping software secure and up to date.

More agents

RolesEach new reviewer or critic adds a role, handoffs and another opinion.

VesselAgents are steps in one loop. Add as many as help; the vessel still owns the outcome.
